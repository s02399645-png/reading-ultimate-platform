# Anchor words for Tests 2–5

An **anchor** is a word in the question that you can hunt for in the passage: a name, a number or date, a limiting word (only, all, never, first), or a distinctive noun.
For every question below you get its anchor words, the place in the passage to look, and a confidence level.

How these were made: the anchors and locations were worked out by a program from your files and the answer keys, then spot-checked. They were **not all read by hand**.

- **high** – the answer words appear in that sentence, or the match is strong. Safe to trust.
- **med** – the match is good but another sentence is close. Worth a glance.
- **low** – usually Not Given / No questions (there is no matching sentence by definition) or very general wording. Check these yourself.
